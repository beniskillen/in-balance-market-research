What this changes in the current plan

Five gaps. Only one belongs in the first test.

The draft objectives on the In Balance page are still drafts. The immediate list is the first 30 days in the living growth plan: reconcile cash, trace one journey, then change one thing. The players below are read against that list, not against a wish to look like Clean Health.

The sentence for the founders

The missing piece is the step between the feeling and the payment.

Keep

The principles, the room, and the refusal to promise a salary.

That is the difference from Certificate IV and from Breathe. The career-change colleges win the “start this week” comparison on price and on a job title gyms already hire. They do not win “I want to teach this, on a reformer, with someone watching”.

Measure

One month of cash, and which problem the lead actually had.

That is Q1. Days 1–14. The 2.27% and the 18 online enrolments are the baseline the test has to beat, on cash, not on a Meta conversion. The account reported 383 conversions and zero campaign leads.

Then test

One clearer next step, on one offer.

Days 15–21, and only if tracking, the payment link and Sammy’s week can hold it. The hypothesis from this chapter: a guide or a conversation will produce a fitter lead than another intake signup. If it only produces more browsing, stop it.
