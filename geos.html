How the money is split

In person shares the result.

The interview’s model: about ten partners, a profit share on the room, and the online sale kept in full. That is why online can be a small enrolment count and still be the strategic opening. It is also why a blended return on ad spend hides the only line that does not pay a partner.

Meta spend classified as in-person was about A$56,100 of A$62,700. Online campaign spend was about A$6,600. September’s online Meta spend fell to A$443. The offer was quiet in the month the industry uses to set up the end of the year.

What not to do with a map

Do not open five countries because the footer lists them.

A room needs a partner, a date, and seats. An online country needs a language, a recognition story, and a page that matches the search. AUSactive is an Australian registration. ICREPs is the international line. Whether a studio in Munich hires on it is still an open question.
