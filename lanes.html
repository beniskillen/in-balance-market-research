Sophistication

The career-change promise is on stage four.

Stage one would be “pilates exists”. Australia is past that. Pilates is a top fitness trend, studios are numerous, and several schools already say “change your career”. A claim without a mechanism bounces off. The mechanism In Balance can actually demonstrate is on the site already: principles before sequences, a person in front of you, assessment, and a qualification a local registry recognises.

Say this

You will understand the body in front of you.

That is a teaching claim. It can be shown in a class video. It does not require an income promise.

Do not say this

You will replace your salary.

The occupation is mostly part-time, and the median full-time week is A$1,500. Breathe said the income line. Breathe is in liquidation.

Test this

Separate the four problems in the form.

The intent field — browsing, ready, or a call with Sammy — is already captured. It is not yet a Meta signal or a segment report. That is the cheapest way to find out which hypothesis is real.
