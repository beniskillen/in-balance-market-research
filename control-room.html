The working file.

This is the room behind the brief. Every planning number in the other chapters points here. If a cell says unknown, it was left unknown. The chapters are a reading version of this ledger, not a second set of facts.

Frame

The decision, and the constraint.

Decision

Which problem is paying, and whether online Australia is the next test.

Not “is pilates growing”, and not “which new country”. Category hypothesis: a person at a life transition, or already in movement, gets a mat and reformer qualification they can teach from, through a principles-led course that exists in a room and online. Out of category: Certificate IV, the government diploma, and a promise of income.

Constraint

Measurement, not a proven lack of demand.

The dashboard’s own first priority. 126 enrolment records and about A$291,000 of recorded value cannot yet be tied to settled cash, a unique new customer, a campaign, or a contribution after partner cost. Demand may be fine. The books cannot say.

Still open

What would change the recommendation.

Would raise the online bet

A reconciled month where online contribution is positive after fees, and the buyer was not already going to a room.

Until then the 18 enrolments are a signal of an underfed offer, not a proven one.

Would change the message

A count of the intent field, plus one new question, showing one problem above the others.

Career change, already teaching, or curious. If they split evenly, one audience in the ad account is the mistake.

Would stop a scale conversation

Settled cash and partner fees that pull the 4.4× media return under water.

Two locked commissions are A$7,000. That is not the cost line. Deposits and the A$825 October plan amount are not full fees.
