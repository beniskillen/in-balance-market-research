Both ends of the offer

The middle is a certificate. The ends are a job title and a diploma.

A short government fitness course is cheap and widely hired. A diploma is long, expensive, and aimed at a fully equipped studio. In Balance is neither. That is a position only if the page says what the course is for, and what it refuses to be.

Volume end · Certificate IV

About A$1,000 to A$2,200, and a job title gyms already use.

Enrolments went from 1,740 to 11,765 in four years, on the cited NCVER series. The person comparing In Balance with this course is not confused. They are choosing a career shape. Pilates training loses that comparison on price and on “will a gym hire me”. It wins on “I want to teach this, in a studio”.

Premium end · the diploma

A$9,000 and up, and the hours are the product.

National Pilates Training prices the diploma at A$9,185 online and A$10,685 in person, over 12 to 18 months. Polestar sells the mat and reformer series as partial diploma units and then adds mentor costs. In Balance’s combined in-person fee, A$4,999 to A$5,999, is a different length of education. Do not let an ad imply they are the same object.

What the collapse changes

Breathe’s absence is a trust event, not a free pile of students.

A school that promised a career, online, at scale, has stopped. Former students are being offered bridging by other providers. Some of that demand can move. It will move toward the school that can say, in plain language, what happens if the student is halfway through, and what the qualification is worth in a studio. In Balance can say the second thing in Australia, because of AUSactive. It has not published the first thing, because it is not the school that closed.

The useful creative lesson from Breathe’s April 2026 walkthrough is the buyer they described, not the guarantee they offered. People at a transition. A reason that survives a hard month. A love of the class. A willingness to be coached. Those sentences match the In Balance interviews. The income language does not survive the liquidation, or the wage data.
