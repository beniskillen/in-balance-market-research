## Decision and constraint

Decision: which of the four buyer problems is actually paying, and whether the online mat-and-reformer offer deserves one Australian test before any new country.

Constraint: measurement. Recorded value and enrolments exist. Settled cash, contribution after delivery, new-versus-repeat customers, and a counted problem set do not. This is not a proven demand problem.

Category hypothesis: a person at a life transition, or already in movement, gets a mat and reformer qualification they can teach from, through a principles-led course that already exists in a room and online.

Out of category: Certificate IV in Fitness, the government pilates diploma, and any promise that the course replaces a salary.

## Buyer hypotheses

1. Career change after a life event. Interview. Not counted in the 126.
2. Already loves the class. The site says this. 1.63 million is the ocean, not the harbour.
3. Already in movement, adding a tool. Online page says this. 35,900 is the occupation, not the slice.
4. Wants clients, not a certificate. Discovery call and the dead Breathe pitch. No graduate outcome is measured. Do not promise income. Full-time median for a fitness instructor is A$1,500 a week, and 68% are part-time.

Sophistication: the career-change promise is worn. Pilates is a top industry trend. Mechanism In Balance can show: principles, a person in front of you, assessment, AUSactive in Australia. ICREPs as a hiring standard outside Australia is untested.
