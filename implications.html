What to feed the dashboard

Keep the market large. Keep the next decision small.

The long view is a pilates school that can also be the considered choice inside a fitness career change. The next decision is not a new country and not a new qualification. It is to find out which of the four problems is actually paying, and to stop scoring the business on a blended click.

Hold

In Balance is a pilates faculty with a career-change door.

Do not rebrand it as a general fitness college. Certificate IV is a neighbouring market with its own price and its own hiring logic. Entering it would be a new company.

Hold

The diploma buyer is not today’s buyer.

Polestar and National Pilates own the long hour-count. In Balance owns a faster, principles-led mat and reformer qualification. Say that difference out loud.

Open

Online is the line the partners do not share.

Eighteen enrolments, about A$6,600 of Meta spend with that name on it, and ads in three countries. That is an underfed offer, not a failed one. It is not yet a proven one either.

The long view, stated as a hypothesis

Win the considered pilates teacher. Then teach them how to work.

Year one stays inside the current qualification: Australia in person, the partner rooms that already have dates, and an online course aimed at people the rooms cannot reach. The career-change message is allowed, because the interviews and the dead competitor both say it is why people enquire. It is not allowed to promise an income.

Year two is a second product, only if graduates can be seen. Completion, a second payment, and a reason code for referral are the gates. The second product is not a diploma and not a Certificate IV. It is the missing piece both the call and the market keep pointing at: how a new teacher gets the first clients, inside the In Balance standard. Circle is the room for that. It is not yet the product. About 300 people joined from about 5,000 invites.

The places that stay closed until that is true: Spain as a campus, cold European online, and any country where ICREPs has not been checked against how a studio actually hires. Bali and Oslo stay as partner intensives, judged on their own seats and their own currency, not on an Australian cost per lead.

Day 90

One reconciled month, and a split of buyers by problem.

If career-change is the majority, the ads should sound like a life event. If trainers are the majority, the ads should sound like a tool. If the split is even, the account should stop using one audience.

Scale

Only the online offer, and only in Australia, if the test clears contribution.

Then New Zealand, because it is already a target and already has a room. The Netherlands is third, because a little search spend already exists and the clicks were cheap for a reason that is not yet known.

Stop

If the reconciled month shows the return was deposits, discounts and partner fees.

Then the work is the offer and the sales conversation, not more media. Sammy’s own line still stands: a lot of the leads were window shoppers.

Internal read. Not a scope and not a media plan.
