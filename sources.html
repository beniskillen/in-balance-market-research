Method. This run follows the Growth Labs market research sequence: frame the decision, separate the market from the category, audit the offer that exists, place competitors at both ends, and size from the bottom as well as the top. The interactive matrix follows the same reading order as the Growth Labs reachable-world matrix: place, then problem, then the offer, with overlapping pools labelled as estimates. It is branded as In Balance, using the live theme’s type and colour. It is an internal file. Publishing it, or putting client figures into a shared Notion page, needs a separate yes.
